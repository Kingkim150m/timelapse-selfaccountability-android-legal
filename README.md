# Timelapse: Adaptive Blocking — legal information

This site contains the Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions for the Timelapse: Adaptive Blocking Android app.

Timelapse stores screen captures, detailed session records, schedules, and settings on your device. Optional Firebase Analytics signals are sent to Google only after you grant the separate Analytics choice. When Website Access is active, permitted domain lookups are sent over encrypted DNS-over-TLS to Cloudflare's public resolver. Google Play processes subscription transactions. A support report is prepared locally and reaches an email or share destination only after you choose to send or share it. The Privacy Policy describes these flows, their controls, and the limits of current retention and deletion handling.
