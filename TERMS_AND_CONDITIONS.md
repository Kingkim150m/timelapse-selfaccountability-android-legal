## 9. Built-in protections and locks

Some protections in the App are built in or automatic rather than optional. By using the App you accept them. They exist to help you keep your own commitments, and you remain responsible for using the App safely.

- **Social Cooldown.** A built-in minimum standard that you agree to during setup. TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X, Reddit, Snapchat, and YouTube share one time allowance, followed by a break. It cannot be turned off, it keeps working when Master Focus is off, and it relies on Usage Access to count your time.
- **Adaptive Protection.** The App may automatically add stronger protection on your device, such as late-night time limits or a temporary block of additional distracting apps after a block. It only makes protection stronger, runs locally, is recorded in the Protection Log, and has no global off switch. You can add limited exceptions where the App allows it.
- **Night Lock and Morning Guard.** A Night Lock blocks apps that are not on its allowed list and can never allow social, video, or game apps. After every enabled Night Lock ends, Morning Guard keeps those apps blocked for 15 more minutes. Morning Guard cannot be turned off or changed.
- **Quick Lock.** A Quick Lock is a countdown lock that you start yourself. Once it is running, you cannot stop it early from within the App; you can only block more apps. Choose a duration you are comfortable with before you start. A Quick Lock can end early only through an Emergency pass, an approved pause, or an Android-level interruption such as force-stopping the App, clearing its data, or the system stopping its service, which the App records. You confirm a "Before you continue" notice each time you start a Quick Lock or turn on a schedule. While a schedule or Quick Lock is active, you generally cannot weaken it from within the App.
- **Emergency pass.** Where enabled for a block, an Emergency pass cancels all active blocks for 24 hours and then needs 60 days to recharge. It does not lift Social Cooldown, and its use is recorded. It is meant for real emergencies.
- **Strict Mode.** An optional layer that protects requests for more time, disabling a schedule, turning off Always-on, and turning off Strict Mode with a passcode held by a trusted contact. Choose a trusted contact only if they know about and agree to hold the passcode; they get no access to your data or device. The App stores the passcode only on your device as a hash and keeps no server copy. The App has no passcode reset button; its built-in recovery is a countdown of up to 60 days that you start from within the App. Agree on the passcode with your trusted contact before you turn Strict Mode on.
- **Protection Log.** The App records protection events locally in the Protection Log, such as permission changes, interruptions, Emergency pass use, Strict Mode events, and Social Cooldown and Adaptive Protection actions.

These protections can block apps you want to use. You accept that risk. Do not rely on the App for safety, and keep another way to reach emergency services and essential contacts.
